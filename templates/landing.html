What's inside

Every tool you need, nothing you don't.

Built for people who want real progress tracking — not gamification gimmicks.

✅

Habit Tracking

Track daily or weekly habits with streak calculations, 7-day row views, and a GitHub-style contribution heatmap for month and year overviews.

📅

Weekly Timetable

Define your own time blocks and fill in a personal reference schedule for each day of the week. Saves automatically, no page reloads.

📊

Stats & Analytics

Compare habit completion counts across custom time ranges (7D / 30D / All Time) with a readable bar chart and per-habit callouts.

🎯

Habit Goals

Set long-term sub-goals for each habit — separate from daily check-ins. Track progress with circular checkboxes and inline editing.

📝

Private Journal

Log daily journal entries with mood tracking and full-text search. Your thoughts stay private and scoped to your account only.

📈

Deep Analytics

Analyse habit completion rates, mood trends over time, and streaks from a dedicated analytics dashboard with exportable data.
